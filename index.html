ABOUT

I am a planetary scientist specialising in numerical modelling of impact processes. In my research group, we use hydrocodes to simulate the formation of impact craters and large basins throughout the solar system.

My work focuses on understanding how high-velocity impacts shape planetary surfaces — from the Moon and Mars to the icy moons of the outer solar system. By running large-scale simulations, we can reconstruct events that occurred billions of years ago and gain insight into the early history of our solar system.

I am passionate about computational geoscience, open science, and making complex research accessible to a broader audience.

RESEARCH

01

Impact Cratering

Numerical simulation of crater formation across planetary bodies, from simple craters to multi-ring basins.

02

Hydrocode Modelling

Development and application of shock physics codes to study high-velocity impact events and material behaviour under extreme conditions.

03

Planetary Evolution

Investigating how impacts have influenced the geological and thermal evolution of planets and moons in our solar system.
